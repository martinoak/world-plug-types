# 🔌 World Plug Map

**Which power plug do I need?** An interactive world map showing the electrical plug types, voltage and frequency used in every country.

### 👉 [Open the map](https://martinoak.github.io/world-plug-types/)

🌍 **Live site:** https://martinoak.github.io/world-plug-types/

---

## Features

- **Hover a country** to see its plug types, voltage and frequency at a glance
- **Click for details**: a drawing and description of each plug type, and where else it's used
- **Search** by country name, including common alternatives like "USA", "UK" or "Czech Republic"
- **Filter by plug type** (A to O) to highlight every country that uses it
- **Zoom and pan** around the world, with light and dark mode and support for phones

## Plug types at a glance

| Type | Description | Common in |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| A | Two flat parallel pins | North & Central America, Japan |
| B | Two flat pins plus a round grounding pin | North & Central America, Japan |
| C | Two round pins (Europlug) | Europe, Asia, Africa, South America |
| D | Three large round pins in a triangle | India, Nepal, parts of Africa |
| E | Two round pins plus a grounding hole | France, Belgium, Poland, Czechia |
| F | Two round pins with side grounding clips (Schuko) | Germany, most of Europe, Russia |
| G | Three rectangular pins | UK, Ireland, Gulf states, Singapore |
| H | Three pins in a Y shape | Israel, Palestine |
| I | Two angled flat pins plus a grounding pin | Australia, New Zealand, China |
| J | Three round pins, middle one offset | Switzerland, Liechtenstein |
| K | Two round pins plus a half-round grounding pin | Denmark, Greenland |
| L | Three round pins in a line | Italy, Chile |
| M | Three large round pins (bigger than D) | South Africa, Lesotho |
| N | Three round pins in a compact plug | Brazil, South Africa |
| O | Two round pins plus a round grounding pin below | Thailand |
